How to play

  1. Choose a starting page and a destination page.
  2. Follow article links in the built-in Wikipedia reader. Search and external links don't count as moves.
  3. Reach the destination in as few clicks and as little time as possible.
  4. For a race, create a room and send your friend its code or invite link. The clock starts when they join.

Wikipedia article text and links load from the English Wikipedia API. A run can also be played solo.
